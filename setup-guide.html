One-time setup · about 5 minutes
Connect your Google Sheet to the scanner
You will add a small piece of code to your Google Sheet. Google then gives you a private link. You paste that link into the scanner, and the scanner can read your INVOICE, INVOICEITEMS, UPCS and SERVICESSOLD tabs and write scans to a new SCAN LOG tab. Do this on a computer, not a phone.
1
Open the code editor inside your sheet
Open your Google Sheet (the one with the INVOICE and UPCS tabs). In the menu at the top, click Extensions, then click Apps Script. A new browser tab opens.

Give permission (first time only)

7
Copy the link Google gives you
A box appears titled "New deployment" with a Web app URL. Click Copy under it. The link starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and ends with /exec. Paste it somewhere safe (an email to yourself works).
Web app URL https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy…long code…/exec Copy
8
Paste the link into the scanner
Open the scanner, tap Settings (top right), paste the link into the box and tap Connect. The line under the title changes to Connected · number of invoices · number of UPCs. You only do this once per phone.

If something goes wrong
"Could not load the sheets" in the scanner: check that step 5 says Anyone, and that the link ends in /exec.
I changed the code later: click Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil icon → Version: New version → Deploy. The link stays the same.
Camera won't start: allow camera access when the phone asks. On iPhone use Safari, on Android use Chrome.
Privacy: anyone who has the link can read the sheet data, so don't share it.
